# Bounded inbound-demand policy

This is a Cascade-only internal triage simulation. Read actual message bodies and relevant attached/standalone agreements, delivery registers and finance support. Subject labels and custodial folders are not demand proof. An earlier letter acknowledgement is not the original letter. Locate the substantive North Fork purchaser request designated by this task; do not assert that no request exists from a search miss or substitute a different purchaser's request.

Use purchaser identity and executed agreement date to join documents. Preserve draft/signed differences; a template or filename alone does not establish executed terms. Use actual agreement clauses as supplied contract text, not current-law validation. A source-labeled signature is not independently authenticated by this benchmark.

Distinguish the sender's requested relief from the contract's election actor. Do not infer Disallowance, timely supported request, notice date, or specified delivery method from sending an unquantified email. Record contractual intervals separately from calendar dates; leave a computed due date null when a qualifying trigger/receipt remains unestablished. No statute or rule may be imported from memory.

Keep source credit face, discounted purchase price, funded cash, fees, buyer slices and possible disallowed scope separate. Delivery/reference reconciliation is not an eligibility determination; neither cash paid nor face amount alone is current refund liability or a reserve. A quantitative settlement choice is not supplied.

Where merits and procedural predicates remain incomplete, the supported next step is an acknowledgement/document-request draft for counsel review. Do not decide a final merit rating, assert settlement protection from a rights-reservation label, authorize payment, send or calendar anything. Preserve existing task-provided context; a proposed link to the one Cascade matter does not certify historical matter creation or conflict clearance.

Source facts may use attested institutional suffixes and D Liu/D. Liu; categorical answer codes are explicitly listed in the public schema. Dates use ISO YYYY-MM-DD. The message_dated_on field records the email header date, not independent proof of contractual receipt. Contract section values use the section number alone from the public choices. Missing/unverified extraction is a coverage limitation, never proof of evidence absence.
