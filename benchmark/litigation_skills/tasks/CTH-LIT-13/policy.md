# Supplied rules for simulated first-pass privilege review

Apply only the supplied Cascade protocol projections. These are benchmark rules, not independently verified current law or a representation of the forum's actual law. Court-specific log requirements, controlling authority and conflicts clearance are not established here. Produce local draft review recommendations only; do not produce, withhold, disclose or serve any document or change a designation.

- **P-ACP:** Assess confidential communication, privileged persons and primary legal-advice purpose separately. Ordinary business/financial correspondence, administrative transmittals and unanalyzed factual reporting do not become legal advice merely because an attorney receives them. Source: Attorney_Client_Privilege.docx ¶26–36.
- **P-ROLES:** CTH counsel and outside-counsel attorneys/agents may communicate with the client in their legal roles. A separate program participant is not automatically within CTH's legal-advice circle. The supplied rules identify Alder Point and Bellhaven as outsiders; do not invent common-interest arrangements. Source: same ¶38–49.
- **P-FAMILY:** Assess each email and attachment independently. A substantive cover may differ from an administrative retention document or preexisting factual/business attachment. A banner does not substitute for analysis. Source: same ¶52–53, ¶61–62, ¶70–73. Do not assume an attachment mentioned in quoted text is actually present or inspected.
- **P-AGENT:** A nonlawyer agent must be retained by counsel (or at its direction), facilitate counsel's legal advice and act within that role. Independent audit, tax and business services do not meet that test merely because the vendor discusses the program or talks to a lawyer. Source: same ¶76–81.
- **P-WP:** Assess attorney-client privilege and work product separately. Work product requires preparation because of anticipated litigation/government proceedings, rather than ordinary business. Attorney authorship is unnecessary. Fact work product compiles factual investigation; opinion work product reflects counsel's mental impressions or strategy. Source: Work_Product.docx ¶24–27.
- **P-TIME:** The supplied protocol uses February 24, 2023 as a bright-line anticipation trigger. A later report/forwarding date does not change an underlying item's creation date or purpose. Before that date, an item is not work product under this protocol, although it may be legal advice. After that date, preparation purpose still matters. Source: same ¶29–33, with case examples excluded from public input.
- **P-UNCERTAIN:** An ambiguous financial analysis must be flagged for attorney review when litigation purpose versus ordinary financial reporting is unresolved. Do not transfer purpose or direction from a different workbook. Keep candidate designations pending human review and identify what evidence would settle the question. Source: same ¶49–51, with named example excluded.
- **P-SHARING:** Assess actual recipients/sharing separately from questions about a proposed disclosure. A direction not to send material establishes advice and risk management, not proof that sending occurred. Sharing with an adversary/outside defense circle is material under the supplied waiver rules; do not invent dissemination or make a definitive real-law waiver conclusion. Source: same ¶47–48, ¶55–56 and ACP ¶28, ¶45–49.
- **P-RESTRAINT:** Preserve source history and unknowns. A missing MIME attachment or extraction failure is not proof that the underlying file never existed. Explicitly distinguish inspected text, quoted descriptions, unresolved purpose and unsupported conclusions. All artifacts are draft recommendations; attorney review is required before a consequential designation or disclosure.

Citations must point to retrieved source content, including sufficient context for the proposition. A sender name or privilege heading alone cannot establish purpose. Citations to an agent's engagement and the specific communication must establish both role and scope; a general retainer alone does not protect every vendor communication. Equivalent supporting passages in original and quoted-thread records can support the same narrow proposition when actually read.
